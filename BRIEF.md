# BRIEF: Amit Kashyap, cinematic 3D scroll portfolio (step 1 scaffold)

Brief source: Amit's own concept described on a voice call (30 Oct 2026), plus his CV (March 2026).
Authored decisions are labelled.

1. Vibe: dark and premium (Amit's words, same as his existing portfolio). Palette and type carried over: #0b0a09 canvas, #c9a36a gold, Cormorant Garamond + Manrope.
2. Journey (Amit's sequence): a child sits facing the screen; scrolling clears fog off the screen; the child rides a bicycle forward; along the road are his companies; then skills; then contact.
3. Energy (authored): calm and intimate at the start, building pace on the ride, a quiet glance at each stop, then stillness at the close.
4. Feeling curve (authored):
   - Frost: curiosity. Something is behind the glass.
   - Wipe: delight. The child is clearing it from his side (his hand sweeps the glass).
   - Mount + ride: momentum. The camera cranes behind him and the road opens.
   - Stops: recognition. Each studio lights up as he passes.
   - Skills/tools: confidence. Big type, still frame.
   - Contact: resolve. "Let's build the next stop."
5. Peak: the frost wipe. "The kid on the other side of the screen wiped the fog off and then rode off on his bike."
   Tell-someone: "It's the site where a kid wipes your screen clean and cycles you through Amit's career."
6. Range: premium-minimal, dark.
7. One unbroken world (Amit chose a continuous journey), so a chained camera is justified here.
8. Assets: none for 3D yet. Child, bike and landmarks are primitive placeholders (to be replaced by GLB models).

Signature move: the frost overlay (shader) clears in a ragged sweep tied to scroll while the child's arm wipes in sync, and the pointer melts a local clear patch.
Note: scroll-craft discourages low-poly worlds as a default; it is used here only as placeholder geometry until real models exist.
